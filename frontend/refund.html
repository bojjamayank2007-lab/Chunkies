REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancellations

You may cancel an order before it is confirmed by the restaurant. Once the order status changes to "Preparing", cancellation may not be possible.

Refunds

Refunds are issued for cancelled orders or if the restaurant fails to deliver your order. Refunds for online payments are credited back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Cash on Delivery

For COD orders, no refund is required unless payment was collected in advance or the order was not delivered.

Quality Issues

If you receive an incorrect or damaged item, contact us within 30 minutes of delivery with your order ID and photo evidence. We will review and resolve on a case-by-case basis.

Contact

For refund or cancellation requests, contact support@chunkies.com or call 090040 94979.
