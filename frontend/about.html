WELCOME TO CHUNKIES

Big flavour. Big chunkies.

Your Go-To Spot for Serious Cravings

At CHUNKIES, we believe in big flavours and even bigger satisfaction. Located in the heart of Jogeshwari West, Mumbai, we're your destination for loaded burgers, crispy chicken, delicious wraps, and sides that hit the spot every time.

Whether you're dining in, grabbing takeaway, or ordering delivery, we've got you covered with food that's made fresh and packed with flavour. From our signature Mexican Chicken Burger to our crispy Chicken Strips, every item on our menu is crafted to satisfy your cravings.

We're open 24 hours, because hunger doesn't follow a schedule. Come visit us at Chunkies Shop on Swami Vivekanand Road, or order from the comfort of your home. Good food, great prices, and flavours that keep you coming back – that's the CHUNKIES promise.
